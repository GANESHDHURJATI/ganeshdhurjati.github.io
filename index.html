01About me

I care about what technology can change—not just what it can do.

I'm Ganesh Dhurjati, a Computer Science student at Georgia Tech concentrating in Intelligence and People. I’m most energized by engineering that leaves the screen and has to work in the real world: software that interprets sensor data, helps a robot understand its environment, supports healthcare teams, or turns a complicated workflow into something people can actually use.

01 / THE WORK

Systems, end to end.

My interests span algorithms, intelligent systems, robotics, automation, backend architecture, and full-stack development. I like understanding the whole system—from data and models to interfaces and deployment—and I care about writing robust software rather than stopping at a proof of concept.

02 / THE WHY

Technology with leverage.

Working on humanoid robotics and deploying connected-health systems in clinical environments made the value of reliable engineering tangible to me. Long term, I want to combine engineering judgment with business and financial insight to build and grow technology companies, especially in healthcare and automation.

03 / BEYOND CODE

Rhythm, patience, detail.

Outside engineering, I’m a sports fan and a musician who plays violin and guitar. Music has trained the same habits I bring to technical work: listen closely, practice deliberately, stay patient when progress is uneven, and notice the small details that change the entire result.
